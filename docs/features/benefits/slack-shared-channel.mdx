A **Shared Slack Channel benefit** creates a dedicated Slack channel for each customer in your workspace and shares it with the customer's own workspace through [Slack Connect](https://slack.com/connect). Use it for premium support or account management.

- One new channel per customer who gets the benefit
- The channel is shared with the customer's workspace, so they don't join yours
- Channels can be archived automatically when the benefit is revoked

<Note>
  The Shared Slack Channel benefit is in preview. Access is granted per organization. [Contact support](/support) to request it.
</Note>

## What the customer gets

A shared channel with your team, named from your template, with your team members already in it.

## Create it

Connecting a Slack app happens in the dashboard. Once it's connected, you can also create benefits from the CLI or your agent.

### Connect a Slack app

Polar posts as a Slack app you create in your own workspace:

- Open **Catalog** → **Benefits**
- Click **Create Benefit** and choose **Shared Slack Channel** as the **Type**
- Under **Slack app**, choose **Create new Slack app**
- Click **Create app on api.slack.com**, choose "From an app manifest", pick your workspace and paste the manifest Polar shows
- Copy the app's **App ID**, **Client ID**, **Client Secret** and **Signing Secret** from its Basic Information page into Polar
- Click **Save and authorize** and approve the app in Slack

You can reuse the same Slack app across benefits.

### Create the benefit

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Dashboard">
    - Select your **Slack app**
    - Set the **Channel name template**, e.g. `support-{customer_email_local}`
    - Choose **Team members to invite**
    - Optionally write a **Welcome message**
    - Click **Create**
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="CLI">
    ```bash
    polar benefits create --data '{
      "type": "slack_shared_channel",
      "description": "Shared Slack channel",
      "properties": {
        "slack_integration_id": "<slack_integration_id>",
        "channel_name_template": "support-{customer_email_local}",
        "team_invitees": ["U012AB3CD"]
      }
    }'
    ```

    Copy `slack_integration_id` from an existing Shared Slack Channel benefit with `polar benefits get <id>`. See [`polar benefits`](/integrate/cli/reference/benefits).
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Prompt">
    ```text Prompt
    Create a Shared Slack Channel benefit using my connected Slack app, with channels named support-{customer_email_local}, in my Polar sandbox organization.
    ```

    Uses the `benefits_create` operation of the [Polar MCP server](/integrate/mcp).
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Options

| Dashboard | API property | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Slack app** | `properties.slack_integration_id` | Required |
| **Channel name template** | `properties.channel_name_template` | Required, up to 80 characters |
| **Create channel as private** | `properties.private` | `true` |
| **Team members to invite** | `properties.team_invitees` (Slack user IDs) | None |
| **Welcome message (optional)** | `properties.welcome_message` | None, up to 4,000 characters |
| **Archive channel on revocation** | `properties.archive_on_revoke` | `true` |

### Channel name template

Every customer's channel follows the same template. It supports these placeholders:

- `{customer_name}`: the customer's name
- `{customer_email_local}`: the part of the customer's email before the `@`
- `{metadata.<key>}`: a value from the customer's metadata, e.g. `{metadata.company}`

`support-{customer_email_local}` produces `support-jane` for `jane@example.com`. Names are converted to lowercase Slack-safe slugs and cut to 80 characters. Polar rejects templates with other placeholders.

## Customer experience

When a customer gets the benefit, the checkout confirmation page and the [Customer Portal](/features/customer-portal/introduction) ask for the email address of an admin in their own Slack workspace. Polar creates the channel, invites your team members, posts your welcome message and sends a Slack Connect invitation to that admin. Once they accept, the channel appears in their workspace.

## Revocation

When the benefit is revoked, for example when a subscription ends, Polar archives the channel. Turn off **Archive channel on revocation** to keep it open.

## Visibility

Always visible. The customer has to enter their Slack admin's email in the portal, so this benefit can't be hidden.
