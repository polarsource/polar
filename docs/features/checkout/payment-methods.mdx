Polar picks the payment methods for each checkout automatically. What a customer sees depends on the checkout currency, their country, the amount, the type of purchase, and their device or browser.

You don't enable payment methods yourself. There's no setting or plan that unlocks more.

## Cards and wallets

| Payment method | Availability | Minimum |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Credit and debit cards | Every supported currency and country. One-time purchases and subscriptions. | |
| Apple Pay | Safari on an Apple device with Apple Pay set up | |
| Google Pay | Google Chrome, signed in to a Google account with Google Pay set up | |
| Link | Customers who saved their details with Stripe Link | |
| Amazon Pay | Customers in the United States and Germany, in USD | $0.50 |
| Cash App Pay | Customers in the United States, in USD | $0.50 |
| Alipay | Customers in mainland China, Hong Kong and Macao, in CNY or USD. One-time purchases only. | $0.50 in USD |
| WeChat Pay | Customers in mainland China, Hong Kong and Macao, in CNY or USD. One-time purchases only. | $0.50 in USD |

<Note>
  **Wallets need no setup on Polar's hosted checkout.** They appear whenever the customer's device and browser support them. Only [Embedded Checkout](/features/checkout/embed#enable-wallets-in-embedded-checkout) needs domain validation, because the checkout runs on your domain.
</Note>

## Local payment methods

| Payment method | Shown when | Purchase type | Amount |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bancontact | Customer in Belgium, in EUR | One-time purchases | €0.45 minimum |
| EPS | Customer in Austria, in EUR | One-time purchases | €1.00 minimum |
| iDEAL / Wero | Customer in the Netherlands, in EUR | One-time purchases | €0.01 minimum |
| UPI | Checkout in INR, any country | One-time purchases and subscriptions | ₹50 to ₹100,000 |
| Korean cards | Checkout in KRW, any country | One-time purchases and subscriptions | ₩675 minimum |
| Kakao Pay | Checkout in KRW, any country | One-time purchases and subscriptions | ₩675 minimum |
| Naver Pay | Checkout in KRW, any country | One-time purchases and subscriptions | ₩675 minimum |
| Samsung Pay | Checkout in KRW, any country | One-time purchases | ₩675 minimum |

UPI and the Korean methods depend on the currency and the amount. They appear when the checkout is priced in INR or KRW and the amount is within the limits in the table above.

## One-time purchases only

Methods marked "One-time purchases" can't be saved for later charges. Polar hides them whenever the checkout saves a payment method: subscriptions, and checkouts with nothing to pay today, such as a free trial. In those cases the customer sees cards and the reusable methods.

## See which method a customer used

In the dashboard, open **Customers** and select the customer. The **Method** column under **Recent Payments** shows how each payment was made.

From the API, CLI or your agent:

- A Checkout Session's `payment_method_type` is the method the customer paid with.
- A customer's saved methods are listed with `polar customers list_payment_methods <id>` or `list_payment_methods_external <external_id>` (MCP `customers_list_payment_methods`, `customers_list_payment_methods_external`). See [List customer payment methods](/api-reference/current/customers/list-customer-payment-methods).

## Other payment methods

The tables above are everything Polar supports today. Payment methods can't be enabled per organization.

PayPal is the most common request. There's no timeline to share.
