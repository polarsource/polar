The **Customers** section of the dashboard answers three questions: who pays you the most, who's about to leave, and who costs you the most. It has four tabs: **Overview**, **Top Customers**, **At Risk** and **Cost Drivers**.

<Note>
  Dashboard only. The views on this page aren't available from the API, CLI or MCP server. See [From the CLI or your agent](#from-the-cli-or-your-agent) for the closest equivalents.
</Note>

Overview, Top Customers and Cost Drivers have a range selector: **All Time**, **12m**, **3m**, **30d** (the default) or **Today**. Click any row to open that customer.

For a single customer's details, such as their subscriptions, orders, usage and events, see [Customer management](/features/customer-management).

## Overview

- A growth chart with two views: **New** (New Customers per period) and **Total** (Total Customers over time).
- The top five of each list below: **Top Customers** by net revenue, **At Risk** (past due and canceling), and **Cost Drivers** from cost events.

## Top Customers

Customers ranked by **net revenue** from paid orders in the selected range, up to 50.

| Column | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Customer** | Name and email |
| **Orders** | Paid orders in the range |
| **Net Revenue** | What you kept, in USD |

Net revenue subtracts refunds, so a partially refunded order counts only for what's left. Orders in other currencies are converted to USD at the exchange rate of their payment.

## At Risk

Subscriptions that may stop paying soon. There's no range: the list shows their current state.

| Column | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Customer** | Name and email |
| **Product** | The subscribed product |
| **Amount** | The subscription's recurring amount |
| **Status** | **Past due**, or **Cancels** with the date it ends |

Two kinds of subscriptions are listed:

- **Past due** first, largest amount first. A renewal payment failed and Polar is retrying it. See [Failed payments](/features/subscriptions/failed-payments).
- **Canceling** next, soonest first. Active or trialing subscriptions set to cancel at the end of the current period. You can still win these back before they end.

## Cost Drivers

Customers ranked by the costs you've recorded for them in the selected range. Costs come from `_cost` metadata on the events you send. If you don't send costs, this tab is empty. See [Cost insights](/features/cost-insights/introduction#cost-events).

| Column | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Customer** | Name and email, or the external ID |
| **Events** | Their events in the range |
| **Share of Costs** | Their part of your total costs |
| **Cost** | Their total cost |

Cost Drivers needs the `analytics:read` permission. Members without it don't see the tab or the Overview list.

For costs over time, margins and breakdowns by event type or model, use **Analytics → Costs**. See [Cost insights](/features/cost-insights/introduction#where-costs-show-up).

## From the CLI or your agent

These don't reproduce the dashboard views, but return the same underlying data:

| Question | CLI | MCP operation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Which subscriptions are past due? | `polar subscriptions list --status past_due --sorting -amount` | `subscriptions_list` |
| Which subscriptions are canceling? | `polar subscriptions list --cancel-at-period-end --sorting current_period_end` | `subscriptions_list` |
| What does one customer cost me? | `polar metrics get --start-date 2026-11-01 --end-date 2026-11-30 --interval month --customer-id <id> --metrics '["costs","gross_margin"]'` | `metrics_get` |

```text Prompt
List the past due and canceling subscriptions in my Polar sandbox organization, largest first.
```
