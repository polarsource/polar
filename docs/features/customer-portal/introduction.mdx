The **Customer Portal** is a hosted page where your customers manage their purchases from you: subscriptions, invoices, payment methods and benefits. They don't need to email you for any of it.

Every organization has one, at `https://polar.sh/<org-slug>/portal`, or `https://sandbox.polar.sh/<org-slug>/portal` in the sandbox. Customers sign in with their email, or you send them in with a pre-authenticated link. See [Link to the portal](/features/customer-portal/navigate-customers).

## What's in the portal

| Page | What customers see |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | Their subscriptions and the benefits they've been granted, like license keys, file downloads and Discord or GitHub access |
| **Orders** | Order history, invoices and payment receipts |
| **Usage** | Their consumption per meter. Shown when **Show metered usage** is on. |
| **Billing** | Payment methods, billing details, email address, billing managers for teams, and a data export |

For [team customers](/concepts/customers#individuals-and-teams), **Orders** and **Billing** are only shown to members with billing permission: the **Owner** (`owner`) and **Billing Manager** (`billing_manager`) roles. Other members see **Overview** and **Usage**.

## What customers can do

Always available:

- Cancel a subscription, and undo a cancellation before the period ends
- Add a payment method and change the default, the main way to recover from a [failed payment](/features/subscriptions/failed-payments)
- Retry the payment of a failed order
- Download and edit invoices, for example to add a company name, tax ID or billing address
- Download a receipt for each paid order
- Access their benefits
- Owners of a team customer can add billing managers, change roles and remove members

Depending on your [portal settings](/features/customer-portal/settings):

| Capability | Default |
| --- | --- |
| View metered usage | On |
| Change subscription plan | On |
| Manage seats | On |
| Change the number of units | Off |
| Pause and resume subscriptions | Off |
| Change their email address | Off |

Team members invited to a seat claim it from the email they receive, which links to the portal. See [Seat-based pricing](/features/seat-based-pricing).

With [localization](/features/checkout/localization) on, the portal is shown in the customer's language.

## Why it matters

- **Failed payment recovery.** When a renewal fails, the portal is where customers add a new card. Card details never touch your servers.
- **Self-service cancellation.** Some jurisdictions, like California under its [Automatic Renewal Law](https://oag.ca.gov/consumers/auto-renewing-subscriptions), require customers to be able to cancel the way they signed up. The portal covers that.
- **Invoices and receipts.** Customers get and edit their own invoices without a support thread.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I turn off the Customer Portal?">
    No. The portal is always available, so customers can always reach their invoices, cancel their subscriptions and update their payment method. You can [limit what they can change](/features/customer-portal/settings), like plan changes or email edits.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I customize how the portal looks?">
    No. The hosted portal looks the same for every organization. For a branded experience, [build your own portal](/features/customer-portal/custom-portal) on the Customer Portal API.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need to send customers a link to the portal?">
    No. Most emails Polar sends your customers already link to it. You can still link to it from your app. See [Link to the portal](/features/customer-portal/navigate-customers).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens when a customer's payment fails?">
    The customer gets an email with a link to the portal, where they can update their payment method. Polar retries past-due orders when the default payment method changes.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Support customers from the dashboard

Customers use the portal themselves. To help one, open them under **Customers**:

- The **Overview** tab shows their subscriptions, orders and benefit grants, the same things they see in the portal
- **Copy Customer Portal** in the **⋮** menu copies a pre-authenticated portal link, valid for one hour
- Choose what customers can change under **Settings → Customer Portal**. See [Portal settings](/features/customer-portal/settings)

## From the CLI or your agent

The same support tasks from the CLI or an agent:

| Task | CLI | MCP operation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| See what a customer has access to | `polar customers get_state_external <external_id>` | `customers_get_state_external` |
| List a team customer's members | `polar customers members list <customer_id>` | `customers_members_list` |
| List a customer's saved payment methods | `polar customers list_payment_methods <customer_id>` | `customers_list_payment_methods` |

Creating a portal link (a customer session) is API only.
