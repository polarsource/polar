A **payout account** is the external account Polar sends your earnings to. As the [Merchant of Record](/merchant-of-record/introduction), Polar collects the money from your customers, then [pays out your balance](/features/finance/payouts), minus fees, to your payout account. From there, the funds go to your bank account.

Polar uses [Stripe Connect Express](https://stripe.com/connect) for payout accounts.

You need a payout account **before Polar can accept payments on your behalf**. Polar won't collect money from your customers that it can't pay out to you, so a working payout account is part of the [review checklist](/merchant-of-record/review-checklist#connect-a-payout-account).

<Note>Dashboard only. Payout accounts aren't available from the CLI or MCP server. Only Admins and the Owner can manage them.</Note>

## Connect a payout account

1. Open [**Finance → Account**](https://polar.sh/to/dashboard/finance/account).
2. Click **Connect payout account**.
3. Select your country:
    - For a personal account, pick your **country of residence**.
    - For a business, pick the **country of tax residency**.
4. Complete onboarding on Stripe. Stripe asks for your identity, business details if any, and the bank account you want to be paid to.
5. Once Stripe confirms your account, you're ready to receive payouts.

The member who completes onboarding becomes the **owner** of the payout account. They own the underlying Stripe account and are the legal recipient of the funds.

## Manage your payout accounts

From [**Finance → Account → Manage payout accounts**](https://polar.sh/to/dashboard/finance/account) you can:

- **Open in Stripe:** update your bank account and business details on Stripe.
- **Make Active:** switch the organization to another payout account you own that isn't linked to an organization.
- **Add Payout Account:** start onboarding for a new payout account, for example in a different country.
- **Delete:** remove a payout account you no longer use. You can only delete one that isn't linked to an organization, has no pending payouts and has a zero Stripe balance.

Switching to another payout account cancels any payout still [held for review](/features/finance/payouts#payout-statuses) against the previous account. The amount returns to your balance, and you can request it again.

## Country and currency requirements

Stripe Connect requires the bank account to be in the **same country as the business** and to use that country's **local currency**. This is a Stripe and banking network requirement that Polar can't override.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can my company be registered in Ireland but use a US bank account for payouts?">
    No. Stripe Connect requires a bank account in the same country as the business, in the local currency. An Irish company must connect an Irish (EUR) bank account, a US company a US (USD) bank account, and so on.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I use Wise, Payoneer, Revolut or another virtual account?">
    Only if it provides a real bank account in the same country as your registered business, in the local currency, and Stripe accepts it. Most multi-currency accounts don't pass Stripe's verification for Connect payouts. If Stripe rejects the account during onboarding, use a domestic bank account instead.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My business is in country A but I live in country B. Which one do I pick?">
    Pick the country where the business is registered for tax purposes. For a personal account, pick your country of residence.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My country isn't in the list. Can I still receive payouts?">
    No. Polar can pay out to the [supported countries](/merchant-of-record/supported-countries#payouts) only. If yours isn't listed during onboarding, Polar can't pay out there yet.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
