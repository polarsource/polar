Your **balance** is what you've earned through Polar after tax and fees. A **payout** moves that balance to your [payout account](/features/finance/accounts), a Stripe Connect Express account linked to your bank. For how a payment turns into a payout, see [Money flow](/concepts/money-flow).

<Note>Dashboard only. Balance, transactions and payouts aren't available from the CLI or MCP server.</Note>

## Your balance

**Finance → Payouts** shows your **Available Balance**, the amount you can withdraw now. **Finance → Income** lists every transaction that makes up your balance, with its **Gross**, **Fees**, **Tax** and **Net** amounts.

Each payment adds to your balance after:

1. **Sales tax** collected for remittance: VAT, GST, US sales tax and others. Polar pays it to the tax authorities. See [Taxes](/features/finance/taxes).
2. **Polar's fees**, which depend on your plan. Fees are calculated on the total the customer paid, tax included. See [Fees](/merchant-of-record/fees).

Balances are kept in USD. Payments in other currencies are converted at the exchange rate when the transaction happens.

### Fee lines

The **Fees** column breaks each fee out by type:

| Fee | When it applies |
| --- | --- |
| **Payment Fee** | Every payment, at your plan's rate |
| **International Payment Fee** | Cards issued outside the US |
| **Subscription Fee** | Subscription payments, on the Early Member rate only |
| **Dispute Fee** | Each dispute, whatever the outcome |
| **Payout Fee** | Each payout |
| **Cross-border Transfer Payout Fee** | Payouts that need a cross-border transfer, depending on your payout account's country |
| **Active Payout Account Fee** | A payout when you've had no other payout in the previous 30 days |
| **Fee Credit** | Fee credits applied to your fees |

If your account holds fee credits, **Finance → Payouts** shows them under **Fee Credits**. Fees are deducted from your credits first.

To download your transactions as a CSV, see [Export income](/features/finance/taxes#export-income).

### Settlement delay

New transactions are held for **7 days** before they count toward your available balance. This applies by default to organizations created on or after May 12, 2026. Organizations created before that date get their funds without the delay.

The amount still settling shows as **Pending** under your available balance, with the date of the next release. Polar can extend the delay or hold specific transactions, most often to match your refund policy, and sometimes for risk or compliance reasons.

## Withdraw your balance

Payouts are manual. Each one carries fixed fees, so you decide when to withdraw and can batch earnings into fewer, larger payouts.

Before you can withdraw:

- You're an **Admin** or the **Owner** of the organization. See [Team management](/features/team-management#permissions).
- Your [payout account](/features/finance/accounts) is connected and ready.
- Your support email is verified. See the [review checklist](/merchant-of-record/review-checklist#add-a-support-email).
- Your available balance is at or above the [minimum for your currency](#minimum-payout-amounts).
- You haven't requested another payout in the last 24 hours.

To withdraw:

1. Open **Finance → Payouts**.
2. Click **Withdraw**.
3. Check the **Gross Amount**, **Fees Amount** and **Net Amount**.
4. Click **Confirm**.

Polar sends the transfer to your payout account right away. Stripe then pays it out to your bank, which typically takes 4 to 7 business days.

If the modal says **Withdrawals are currently unavailable**, your organization isn't approved for payouts. See [Account reviews](/merchant-of-record/account-reviews#organization-statuses).

### Minimum payout amounts

The minimum balance depends on your payout currency. Some currencies have a higher minimum because of Stripe's requirements, and these limits can change.

| Currency | Minimum balance (USD) |
| --- | --- |
| USD | $10 |
| EUR | $13 |
| GBP, CHF | $15 |
| AOA | $30 |
| ALL, AMD, AZN, BAM, BOB, BTN, CLP, GHS, GMD, GYD, KHR, KRW, LAK, MDL, MGA, MKD, MNT, MYR, MZN, NAD, PYG, RSD, THB, TWD, UZS | $40 |
| COP | $50 |
| Other currencies | $10 |

A few countries have a higher minimum:

| Country | Minimum balance (USD) |
| --- | --- |
| Bahamas | $30 |
| El Salvador | $30 |
| Panama | $50 |

For payout accounts in Icelandic króna (ISK), Hungarian forint (HUF), New Taiwan dollar (TWD) or Ugandan shilling (UGX), Stripe pays out whole currency units only. Any fraction stays in your balance and goes out with your next payout.

### Payout fees

Stripe charges these fees on each payout. Polar passes them on without a markup:

- **Payout fee:** 0.25% + $0.25 per payout.
- **Cross-border transfer fee:** between 0% and 1.25% of the payout, depending on your payout account's country. For example, 0.25% for euro countries and the UK.
- **Active payout account fee:** $2, charged on a payout when you haven't had another payout in the previous 30 days.

The withdraw modal shows the exact fees before you confirm.

If you leave a balance unwithdrawn for several months, Polar may trigger a payout on your behalf.

## Payout statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Pending** | Requested. The transfer to your payout account is being processed. |
| **Held for review** | Requested while your organization is under review. Paid out automatically once the review is approved. |
| **In Transit** | On its way from Stripe to your bank. |
| **Succeeded** | Paid out to your bank. |
| **Failed** | Stripe couldn't complete the payout. |
| **Canceled** | Canceled before the transfer. The amount and its fees return to your available balance. |

A held payout is canceled if the review ends with your organization denied, blocked or offboarded. It's also canceled if you switch to a different payout account while it's held.

## Payout documents

Each payout row on **Finance → Payouts** has a menu with:

- **Download CSV:** the transactions included in that payout.
- **Generate invoice** or **Download invoice:** a reverse invoice, for succeeded payouts.

### Reverse invoices

Polar invoices your customers directly as the [Merchant of Record](/merchant-of-record/introduction). For your own accounting, you invoice Polar for each payout. A **reverse invoice** lists the sales Polar made on your behalf, minus its fees.

When you generate one, you can set:

- **Billing name** and **Billing address**.
- **Additional billing information**, shown below your address. Use it for your VAT number or other details your local rules require.
- **Notes**, shown at the bottom of the invoice.
- **Invoice number**. The default looks like `POLAR-0001`. You can change it to your own format and sequence.

<Warning>
  A reverse invoice can't be changed once it's generated. Check the details before you confirm.
</Warning>

See a [sample reverse invoice (PDF)](https://polar-public-assets.s3.us-east-2.amazonaws.com/sample-reverse-invoice.pdf).

## Tax forms

### 1099-K (US)

Polar issues Form 1099-K through Stripe each year to eligible merchants in the US. Platforms like Polar issue a 1099-K rather than a 1099-NEC or 1099-MISC because they process card payments between buyers and sellers who aren't their contractors. Stripe explains the requirements in its [1099-K guide](https://support.stripe.com/questions/intro-to-1099-k-tax-forms-for-platforms-and-marketplaces).

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="When does a payout reach my bank?">
    Polar starts the transfer to your payout account as soon as you confirm. Stripe then pays it out to your bank, typically within 4 to 7 business days.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How often can I withdraw?">
    Once every 24 hours. If you've recently requested a payout, the withdraw modal shows when you can request the next one.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I use my personal bank account to receive payouts?">
    For individual accounts, yes. For business accounts, you need a business bank account.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does my payout say Held for review?">
    You can request a payout at any time. If your organization is under [review](/merchant-of-record/account-reviews) when you do, the payout is held until the review is approved, then paid out automatically.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
