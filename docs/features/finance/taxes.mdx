As your [Merchant of Record](/merchant-of-record/introduction), Polar collects sales tax on every order and remits it to the tax authorities. You don't register or file anywhere for it. **Finance → Taxes** shows what Polar handled for you, and **Finance → Income** lets you export your transactions for your own bookkeeping.

<Note>Dashboard only. Taxes and the income export aren't available from the CLI or MCP server. Viewing them needs the **Finance**, **Admin** or **Owner** role.</Note>

## Tax remitted by Polar

Open **Finance → Taxes**. The page shows, for the selected date range:

- **Tax remitted by Polar:** the total tax Polar collected and pays on your behalf.
- **Jurisdictions covered:** how many jurisdictions your customers paid tax in, and how many orders that covers.
- **Breakdown:** one row per jurisdiction with its **Orders**, **Status** and **Tax handled**.

The date range defaults to the current month. Change it with the date picker above the breakdown.

| Column | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Jurisdiction** | The country, or the state or province for the US and Canada. Everywhere else is grouped by country. |
| **Orders** | Orders with tax in that jurisdiction |
| **Status** | **Handled**: Polar collects and remits this tax |
| **Tax handled** | Net tax for that jurisdiction, in the currency it was charged in |

Amounts are net: tax returned on refunds and disputes is subtracted, and added back if a refund or dispute is reversed.

<Warning>
  Polar handles sales tax, such as VAT, GST and US sales tax. You're still responsible for income tax on your earnings in your own country.
</Warning>

## Export income

To download your transactions as a CSV:

1. Open **Finance → Income**.
2. Click **Export**.
3. Pick a **Date range**.
4. Select the columns you want.
5. Download the file.

| Group | Columns |
| --- | --- |
| Transaction | **Created At**, **Type**, **Product**, **Status**, **Paid out at**, **Invoice number**, **Order ID** |
| Amounts | **Gross**, **Fees**, **Tax**, **Net**, **Currency** |

All columns except **Invoice number** and **Order ID** are selected by default.

For the transactions included in one payout, use **Download CSV** on that payout instead. See [Payout documents](/features/finance/payouts#payout-documents).
