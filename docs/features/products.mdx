A **product** is anything you sell on Polar: a subscription plan or a one-time purchase. Its prices decide what the customer pays and its [benefits](/concepts/benefits) decide what they get. Read [Products & prices](/concepts/products) for price types and how they combine. This page covers creating and managing products.

The code examples use API version `2026-10` and the sandbox. See [Sandbox](/integrate/sandbox) to get an access token.

## Create a product

A product needs a name, at least one price, and a billing cycle if it's recurring. Amounts are in cents.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Dashboard">
    - Open **Products**
    - Click **New Product**
    - Enter a **Name**
    - Under **Pricing**, choose **Recurring subscription** and set **Every** 1 month
    - Pick **Fixed price** and enter $20
    - Click **Create Product**
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="CLI">
    ```bash
    polar products create --name "Pro" --recurring-interval month \
      --prices '[{ "amount_type": "fixed", "price_amount": 2000 }]'
    ```

    See [`polar products`](/integrate/cli/reference/products).
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Prompt">
    ```text Prompt
    Create a monthly product called Pro for $20 in my Polar sandbox organization.
    ```

    Uses the `products_create` operation of the [Polar MCP server](/integrate/mcp).
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<CodeGroup>
```typescript TypeScript
const product = await polar.products.create({
  name: "Pro",
  recurring_interval: "month",
  prices: [{ amount_type: "fixed", price_amount: 2000 }],
});
```

```python Python
product = polar.products.create(
    name="Pro",
    recurring_interval="month",
    prices=[{"amount_type": "fixed", "price_amount": 2000}],
)
```

```bash cURL
export PRODUCT_ID=$(curl -s -X POST https://sandbox-api.polar.sh/v1/products/ \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $POLAR_ACCESS_TOKEN" \
  -H "Polar-Version: 2026-10" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "name": "Pro",
    "recurring_interval": "month",
    "prices": [{ "amount_type": "fixed", "price_amount": 2000 }]
  }' | jq -r .id)
```
</CodeGroup>

Leave out `recurring_interval` to create a one-time product.

<Visibility for="agents">
  Create products in the sandbox until the user says otherwise. `POST /v1/products/` takes `name`, `prices` and, for a subscription, `recurring_interval` (`day`, `week`, `month` or `year`) with an optional `recurring_interval_count` (1 to 999). Attach benefits afterwards with `POST /v1/products/{id}/benefits`.
</Visibility>

## Billing cycle

A product is a **One-time purchase** or a **Recurring subscription**. A recurring product bills **Every** _n_ days, weeks, months or years, where _n_ is 1 to 999. In the API, that's `recurring_interval` and `recurring_interval_count`.

The billing cycle is fixed once the product is created. To change it, create a new product.

## Pricing

Under **Pricing**, add one or more prices. See [Pricing models](/features/pricing-models) for how they combine into one-time purchases, subscriptions, credits and usage. The dashboard offers these price types:

| Dashboard | API `amount_type` | See |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Fixed price** | `fixed` | [Fixed pricing](/features/fixed-pricing) |
| **Pay what you want** | `custom` | `minimum_amount`, `maximum_amount`, `preset_amount` |
| **Free** | `fixed` with `price_amount: 0` | [Free products](/features/fixed-pricing#free-products) |
| **Seats** | `seat_based` | [Seat-based pricing](/features/seat-based-pricing) |
| **Units** | `unit_based` | [Unit-based pricing](/features/unit-based-pricing) |
| **Metered price** | `metered_unit` or `metered_tiers` | [Usage-based pricing](/features/usage-based-billing/billing). Recurring products only |

Not every combination is allowed. A fixed price combines with a seat-based or a unit-based price, pay what you want and free stand alone, and metered prices stack on top. See [Combining prices](/concepts/products#combining-prices).

Whether an amount includes tax depends on your [tax behavior](/features/tax-inclusive-pricing).

### Variants

Polar has no variants. To sell a monthly and a yearly plan, create two products and offer both at checkout with a [Checkout Link](/features/checkout/links) or a [Checkout Session](/features/checkout/session). Each product keeps its own prices and benefits.

## Multiple payment currencies

A product can have prices in several currencies so customers pay in their local one. Your organization's **Default payment currency** (**Settings → Payments**) is the fallback, and every product needs a price in it. To price a product only in another currency, change the default first.

Every currency on a product must have the same set of price types. If the monthly plan has a fixed and a metered price in USD, it needs both in EUR too. The supported currencies are listed under `price_currency` in the [Create Product](/api-reference/current/products/create-product) reference.

Polar picks the currency from the customer's location at checkout. If that currency isn't on the product, it falls back to the default.

<Warning>
  **Creating Checkout Sessions from a server?** Polar reads the location from the IP address of the request that creates the session. From your backend or a worker, that's your server's IP, and the customer may see the wrong currency. Pass the customer's IP as `customer_ip_address`. See [Checkout Sessions](/features/checkout/session).
</Warning>

## Visibility

`visibility` controls where a product can be bought. Set it under **Customer Portal → Visibility** on the product.

| Value | Dashboard | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `public` | **Public** | Shown in the [Customer Portal](/features/customer-portal/introduction) and available at checkout. The default |
| `private` | **Private** | Only purchasable through a direct Checkout Link or Checkout Session |
| `draft` | | Can't be checked out |

Visibility also limits which products a customer can switch to in a plan change.

## Trial period

A recurring product can start with a free trial. Under **Free Trial Configuration**, click **Configure Trial**, set the **Trial duration** and choose whether to **Require credit card up front**. In the API, set `trial_interval` and `trial_interval_count`.

<Note>
  Trials without a card (`trial_payment_setup_required: false`) are available from API version 2027-01. See [Create Product](/api-reference/next/products/create-product).
</Note>

See [Trials](/features/subscriptions/trials) for how trials start, convert and end.

## Benefits

Benefits are what customers get: license keys, file downloads, Discord roles, GitHub access, feature flags, credits or a custom benefit. Polar grants them on purchase and revokes them when access ends.

Attach benefits with `polar products update_benefits <id> --benefits '["<benefit_id>"]'` or `POST /v1/products/{id}/benefits`. See [Benefits](/concepts/benefits).

## Checkout page

These fields shape how the product appears at checkout:

- **Description**: shown on the checkout page. Markdown is supported.
- **Product images**: JPEG, PNG, GIF, WebP or SVG, up to 10 MB each.
- **Checkout Fields**: [custom fields](/features/custom-fields) you define once for the organization and attach per product, each optional or required. Collected values land on the order or subscription.

## Metadata

Attach key-value `metadata` to a product to store your own IDs or categories. Customers don't see it. It's returned on the product in the API and in webhooks.

## Update a product

Everything except the billing cycle can change after creation, including the price types.

- **Prices.** Send a new `prices` list to replace them. To keep an existing price, include it as `{ "id": "<price_id>" }`. Replaced prices are archived. Existing subscribers stay on the price they signed up at, so a change only affects new purchases. To move a subscriber, [update their subscription](/features/subscriptions/manage).
- **Meter cycle.** A `meter_interval` can't be changed once set. See [Meter cycle](/features/usage-based-billing/billing#meter-cycle).
- **Benefits.** Adding a benefit grants it to existing customers. Removing one revokes it.

To start from a copy, use **Duplicate Product** in the product menu. Duplicating is dashboard only.

## Archive a product

Archive a product to stop selling it. It disappears from new checkouts and its Checkout Links are archived too. Existing customers keep their benefits and subscriptions keep renewing. Unarchive it to sell it again.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Dashboard">
    - Open the product
    - Open the **…** menu
    - Click **Archive Product**
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="CLI">
    ```bash
    polar products update <product_id> --is-archived true
    ```
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Prompt">
    ```text Prompt
    Archive the Pro product in my Polar sandbox organization.
    ```

    Uses the `products_update` operation of the [Polar MCP server](/integrate/mcp).
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Delete a product

A product with no orders, subscriptions, trials or discounts can be deleted for good. Otherwise, archive it.

In the dashboard, open the product's **…** menu and click **Delete Permanently**. From the CLI, run `polar products delete <product_id>`.

## From the CLI or your agent

| Task | CLI | MCP operation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| List products | `polar products list` | `products_list` |
| Create a product | `polar products create` | `products_create` |
| Get a product | `polar products get <id>` | `products_get` |
| Update, archive or change visibility | `polar products update <id>` | `products_update` |
| Attach benefits | `polar products update_benefits <id>` | `products_update_benefits` |
| Delete a product | `polar products delete <id>` | `products_delete` |

The MCP server calls API version 2027-01. See [MCP operations](/agents/mcp-operations).
