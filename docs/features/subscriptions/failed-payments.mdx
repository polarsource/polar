When a subscription renews, Polar starts the next billing cycle, creates the order and charges the customer's saved payment method. If the charge fails, the subscription moves to `past_due` and Polar retries it on a schedule, a process called **dunning**, instead of ending it right away.

## What happens when a renewal fails

1. The subscription moves from `active` to `past_due`, and `past_due_at` records when.
2. Polar emails the customer with a link to the [Customer Portal](/features/customer-portal/introduction), where they can update their payment method or retry the payment.
3. The renewal order stays open, with `next_payment_attempt_at` set to the next retry.

Polar then retries on a fixed schedule, counted from the first failure:

| Retry | Delay from previous | Time since first failure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1st | 2 days | 2 days |
| 2nd | 5 days | 7 days |
| 3rd | 7 days | 14 days |
| 4th | 7 days | 21 days |

If a retry succeeds, the order is paid. The subscription returns to `active` once all its open orders are paid. A `past_due` subscription keeps renewing, so there can be more than one.

If every retry fails, Polar **revokes** the subscription: it moves to `canceled` and benefits are revoked.

When the card is declined in a way that won't recover, such as `lost_card`, Polar skips the retries in between and makes one last attempt at the 21-day mark. The customer can still add a new card before then.

The `subscription.past_due` webhook fires when the subscription enters `past_due`, and `subscription.updated` on every status change. See [Webhook events](/integrate/webhooks/events).

## Benefit revocation grace period

By default, benefits are revoked as soon as the subscription becomes `past_due`. A grace period keeps them granted for a while, so one expired card doesn't lock a paying customer out while they fix it.

Set it under **Settings → Subscriptions → Grace period for benefit revocation**. The API field is `subscription_settings.benefit_revocation_grace_period` on the organization, in days.

| Option | `benefit_revocation_grace_period` |
| --- | --- |
| **Immediately** (default) | `0` |
| **After 2 Days** | `2` |
| **After 7 Days** | `7` |
| **After 14 Days** | `14` |
| **After 21 Days** | `21`, the whole retry schedule |

The grace period counts from `past_due_at`. During it, the subscription is still `past_due`, so your app can tell the difference, but the customer keeps their benefits. When it runs out, or the subscription is revoked, benefits are revoked.

<Note>
  The grace period only delays benefit revocation. It doesn't change the retry schedule or keep the subscription `active`. To treat past-due customers differently in your app, for example with a banner, check for `status === "past_due"` in [customer state](/integrate/customer-state) or the `subscription.updated` webhook.
</Note>

## How customers recover

From the [Customer Portal](/features/customer-portal/introduction), a customer can:

- **Update their payment method.** Polar retries the open orders with the new card right away instead of waiting for the next scheduled attempt.
- **Retry the payment** on the failed order, up to 5 times per order.

Link to the Customer Portal from your app when a customer is `past_due`. See [Customer Portal](/features/customer-portal/introduction) for how to create a portal link.

## What you can do

In the dashboard, open the subscription from **Sales → Subscriptions**. Its **Activity** shows **Payment failed**, each **Retry failed** and the next **Payment retry**.

- **Find past-due subscriptions** and inspect the failed attempts and their decline reasons.
- **[Reschedule the renewal](/features/subscriptions/manage#reschedule-the-next-renewal)** to move the next billing date.
- **[Revoke the subscription](/features/subscriptions/manage#revoke-now)** if you don't want to keep trying.

<Tabs>

## Usage billed on its own order

When a subscription bills metered usage on a separate order at the end of a meter period, a failed payment on that order follows the same retry schedule but doesn't move the subscription to `past_due`. If the retries run out, the order is voided. See [Billing](/features/usage-based-billing/billing).

## Customer emails

Two [customer notification emails](/features/subscriptions/introduction#customer-notification-emails) help prevent and recover failed payments. Both are under **Settings → Customer Notifications → Renewals**:

- **Subscription past due**, sent when a renewal payment fails.
- **Card expiring**, sent before the card backing an active subscription expires.
