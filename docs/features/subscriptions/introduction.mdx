A **subscription** is the recurring relationship between a customer and one of your recurring [products](/features/products). Each renewal creates a new order and charges the customer's saved payment method, until you or the customer end it.

Statuses, renewals and when benefits are revoked are covered in [Orders & subscriptions](/concepts/orders-and-subscriptions). This page covers how subscriptions start and the settings around them.

## How a subscription starts

- **Through checkout.** The default, and the only way for most paid products. A customer completes a [Checkout Session](/features/checkout/session) or [Checkout Link](/features/checkout/links) for a recurring product, and Polar creates the subscription and its first order.
- **Through the API.** [Create Subscription](/api-reference/current/subscriptions/create-subscription) subscribes an existing customer to a **free** recurring product. No order is created and no confirmation email is sent. Use it to give every signup a subscription from day one.

<CodeGroup>
```typescript TypeScript
const subscription = await polar.subscriptions.create({
  product_id: freeProductId,
  external_customer_id: "user_123",
});
```

```python Python
subscription = polar.subscriptions.create(
    product_id=free_product_id,
    external_customer_id="user_123",
)
```

```bash cURL
export SUBSCRIPTION_ID=$(curl -s -X POST https://sandbox-api.polar.sh/v1/subscriptions/ \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $POLAR_ACCESS_TOKEN" \
  -H "Polar-Version: 2026-10" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "product_id": "'"$FREE_PRODUCT_ID"'",
    "external_customer_id": "user_123"
  }' | jq -r .id)
```
</CodeGroup>

The customer must already exist. Pass `customer_id` instead of `external_customer_id` if you store Polar customer IDs.

<Note>API only. Not available from the CLI or MCP server.</Note>

<Note>
  Available from API version 2027-01: [Create Subscription](/api-reference/next/subscriptions/create-subscription) also accepts paid products whose trial doesn't require a card up front. The subscription starts in its trial, and the request returns `409` if the customer already has an active subscription or has already used a trial. See [Trials](/features/subscriptions/trials#card-up-front-or-card-less).
</Note>

## Pricing

A subscription follows the pricing of its product: the billing interval (**Every** N days, weeks, months or years), and fixed, seat, unit or metered prices. See [Products](/concepts/products) for the price types and how they combine.

The billing interval is fixed once a product is created. To offer monthly and yearly plans, create two products. The subscription keeps the currency it was created in.

## Multiple subscriptions per customer

By default, a customer can have one active subscription in your organization. To let customers hold several at once, turn on **Settings → Subscriptions → Allow multiple subscriptions**. The API field is `subscription_settings.allow_multiple_subscriptions` on the organization.

## Renewal reminders

For billing cycles of six months or more, Polar emails the customer 7 days before the renewal. Some regulations require this advance notice, and it heads off chargebacks from forgotten renewals.

These intervals get a reminder:

| Interval | Reminder when the interval count is |
| --- | --- |
| Year | 1 or more |
| Month | 6 or more |
| Week | 25 or more |
| Day | 180 or more |

Free subscriptions and subscriptions set to cancel at period end don't get one. Turn reminders off under **Settings → Customer Notifications → Renewals → Renewal reminder**.

## Customer notification emails

Polar emails your customers when their subscription changes. Each email can be turned off under **Settings → Customer Notifications**, for example when you send your own.

| Group | Email | Sent when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Purchases | **Subscription confirmation** | A subscription starts |
| Trials | **Trial conversion reminder** | A trial is about to end. See [Trials](/features/subscriptions/trials#reminders) |
| Trials | **Trial converted** | A trial ends and the subscription becomes paid |
| Renewals | **Renewal reminder** | A long billing cycle is about to renew |
| Renewals | **Subscription cycled** | A subscription renews or is invoiced for usage |
| Renewals | **Card expiring** | The card backing an active subscription is about to expire |
| Renewals | **Subscription past due** | A renewal payment failed. See [Failed payments](/features/subscriptions/failed-payments) |
| Subscription changes | **Subscription updated** | The subscription moves to a different product |
| Subscription changes | **Subscription paused** / **Subscription resumed** | The subscription is paused or resumed |
| Subscription changes | **Subscription canceled** / **Subscription uncanceled** | A cancellation is scheduled or reversed |
| Subscription changes | **Subscription revoked** | A canceled subscription ends |

Your own team's notifications are separate. See [Notifications](/features/notifications).

## Webhooks

Every change sends a webhook: `subscription.created`, `subscription.active`, `subscription.updated`, `subscription.cycled`, `subscription.past_due`, `subscription.canceled`, `subscription.uncanceled`, `subscription.paused`, `subscription.resumed` and `subscription.revoked`. See [Webhook events](/integrate/webhooks/events).

To check access, read [customer state](/integrate/customer-state) rather than tracking each event.

## Find subscriptions

- Open **Sales → Subscriptions**
- Use **Search subscriptions**, or filter by **Status** or **Cancellation reason**
- Switch between the timeline and table views, and **Sort** by **Newest**, **Next renewal** or **Highest amount**
- Select a subscription to see its orders, scheduled changes and **Activity**

The statuses in the dashboard map to the API's `status`: **Trialing** (`trialing`), **Active** (`active`), **Past due** (`past_due`), **Paused** (`paused`), **Trial ended** (`trial_ended`), **Canceled** (`canceled`), **Incomplete** (`incomplete`) and **Unpaid** (`unpaid`). An active subscription set to end shows as **Canceling**, and one set to pause as **Pausing**. See [Orders & subscriptions](/concepts/orders-and-subscriptions) for what each means.

From the CLI, use `polar subscriptions list`. See [From the CLI or your agent](#from-the-cli-or-your-agent).

## Export subscriptions

Open **Sales → Subscriptions**, then choose **Export CSV** from the actions menu in the toolbar. Pick the columns and download the file.

<Note>Dashboard only. Not available from the CLI or MCP server.</Note>

## From the CLI or your agent

| Task | CLI | MCP operation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| List active subscriptions | `polar subscriptions list --status active` | `subscriptions_list` |
| List a product's subscriptions | `polar subscriptions list --product-id <id>` | `subscriptions_list` |
| Get a subscription | `polar subscriptions get <id>` | `subscriptions_get` |
| Change a subscription | `polar subscriptions update <id>` | `subscriptions_update` |
| End a subscription now | `polar subscriptions revoke <id>` | `subscriptions_revoke` |

See the [CLI reference](/integrate/cli/reference/subscriptions) and the [MCP server](/integrate/mcp).
