## Overview

Metering lets you track how your customers use your application. You ingest events from your application, and Meters aggregate those events into usage per customer.

You can then use that usage to:

- Charge customers for it with [metered prices](/features/usage-based-billing/billing)
- Grant usage [credits](/features/usage-based-billing/credits) that are consumed as usage comes in
- Show customers their usage and estimated charges in the [Customer Portal](/features/customer-portal/introduction)

The same events can also carry cost data, which [Cost Insights](/features/cost-insights/introduction) uses to track what each customer costs you.

    <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/assets/features/usage/product-meter.light.png" />

  </Step>
