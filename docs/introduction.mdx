Polar runs billing for software products. You decide what customers get and how you charge for it. Polar handles access, usage tracking, checkout, invoicing, global sales tax and customer self-service.

Polar is built around one loop.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="1. Enable & track usage" icon="gauge" href="/concepts/usage">
    Grant [benefits](/concepts/benefits) like license keys, feature flags and
    credits, then track what customers use with [events and
    meters](/concepts/usage). Use credit balances and spending caps as
    guardrails.
  </Card>
  <Card title="2. Bill for that usage" icon="file-invoice-dollar" href="/concepts/orders-and-subscriptions">
    Charge for usage on top of subscriptions and one-time purchases. Polar
    creates the [orders](/features/orders), collects payment and handles
    [failed payments](/features/subscriptions/failed-payments).
  </Card>
  <Card title="3. Manage customers" icon="users" href="/concepts/customers">
    Read everything a customer has access to from one [customer
    state](/integrate/customer-state), and let customers manage their own
    billing in the [customer portal](/features/customer-portal/introduction).
  </Card>
  <Card title="4. Iterate on pricing" icon="flask" disabled>
    **Coming soon.** Version your pricing and simulate changes against real
    usage before you roll them out.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Set up Polar with your agent

Everything you can do in the dashboard, you can do from your terminal or AI agent. Create products and benefits, set up checkouts, look up customers and manage subscriptions with the Polar CLI and MCP server.

```bash Terminal
curl -fsSL https://polar.sh/install.sh | bash
polar agent setup
```

`polar agent setup` detects Claude Code, Codex and Cursor and connects them to Polar's MCP server and skills.

## Pricing models

Mix these on a single product, like a fixed base fee plus usage.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Fixed price" icon="tag" href="/features/products#pricing">
    One-time purchases and subscriptions at a set price.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Usage-based" icon="chart-line" href="/features/usage-based-billing/billing">
    Per unit, volume or graduated rates on metered usage.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Credits" icon="coins" href="/features/usage-based-billing/credits">
    Prepaid usage that's drawn down as customers use your product.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Seat-based" icon="user-group" href="/features/seat-based-pricing">
    Per-seat team plans, with seats assigned to members.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Unit-based" icon="cubes" href="/features/unit-based-pricing">
    A quantity of anything, paid up front, with tiered rates.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Pay what you want & free" icon="hand-holding-dollar" href="/features/products#pricing">
    Customer-chosen amounts with a minimum, or free tiers.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Add [trials](/features/subscriptions/trials) and [discounts](/features/discounts) to any of them, price in [multiple currencies](/features/products#multiple-payment-currencies), and choose [tax-inclusive or exclusive](/features/tax-inclusive-pricing) prices.

## Integrate Polar

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your products">
    Model your [products](/concepts/products), prices and benefits in the
    dashboard, with the [CLI](/integrate/cli/introduction), or through the
    [API](/api-reference).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add checkout">
    Share a [Checkout Link](/features/checkout/links), [embed
    checkout](/features/checkout/embed) on your site, or create [checkout
    sessions](/features/checkout/session) from your backend.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sync access">
    Read the [customer state](/integrate/customer-state) or listen to
    [webhooks](/integrate/webhooks/endpoints) to grant access in your
    application.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track usage">
    [Ingest events](/features/usage-based-billing/event-ingestion) for
    anything you meter or draw down from credits.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go live">
    Pass the [account review](/merchant-of-record/account-reviews), connect a
    [payout account](/features/finance/accounts), and switch from the sandbox
    to production.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Polar is your Merchant of Record

Polar is the seller of record for every sale. We calculate, collect and remit sales tax and VAT worldwide, work to prevent chargebacks, and pay out your balance.
