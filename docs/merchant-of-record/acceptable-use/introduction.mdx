## What the policy covers

The **Acceptable Use Policy** (AUP) defines which products and business models you can sell through Polar. Polar is built for software companies, so the policy covers digital goods and services such as:

- Software and SaaS
- Digital products: templates, ebooks, code, icons, fonts, design assets and similar
- Premium content and access: courses, private GitHub repositories, Discord servers and subscription content

It also lists what isn't supported, such as physical goods, human services, marketplaces and high-risk or regulated categories, and the businesses that need a closer review.

## Why it exists

As your [Merchant of Record](/merchant-of-record/introduction), Polar is the legal seller of your products. It's accountable to card networks, payment partners and tax authorities for every transaction. The AUP keeps the platform compliant, protects buyers from fraud and abuse, and keeps dispute and risk levels low so Polar can offer its rates and payouts to everyone.

## When it's checked

Your organization is checked against the AUP when you submit it for review, before you can accept payments. The check is automated and takes a few minutes. Polar then keeps monitoring your organization as you sell. See [Account reviews](/merchant-of-record/account-reviews).
