As your [Merchant of Record](/merchant-of-record/introduction), Polar is the legal seller of your products. So Polar checks that every organization complies with the [Acceptable Use Policy](/acceptable-use) (AUP), and keeps monitoring transactions for fraud, abuse and risk.

An **account review** is that check. The first one runs when you submit your organization, and it's automated: you get a result in a few minutes. Reviews then continue as you sell.

<Note>Dashboard only. Reviews and appeals aren't available from the CLI or MCP server.</Note>

## Your first review

<Tip>
  **Build first, submit second.** Set up your [products and benefits](/features/products), your checkout (Checkout Links or the API with webhooks) and a live website before you submit. The more the review can see end to end, the more likely it passes on the first try.
</Tip>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Complete the checklist">
    Open [**Finance → Account**](https://polar.sh/to/dashboard/finance/account) and complete every required item: a product, a checkout setup, the owner's identity verification, a payout account, a product description, a reachable website and a verified support email. See the [review checklist](/merchant-of-record/review-checklist).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit for review">
    Click **Submit for review**. The checklist locks.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Automated check">
    Polar checks your organization details against the AUP. This typically takes one to two minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Result">
    You see one of the results below.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Result | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Approved!** | You can accept payments immediately. A manual review still takes place. Payouts you request in the meantime are paid out automatically once it's complete. |
| **Additional review required** | The check couldn't verify your organization automatically, so a person reviews it. You can't accept payments yet. [Submit an appeal](#appeals) to speed up the manual review. |
| **Payment access denied** | Your organization doesn't meet the AUP, or the check hit a technical error and a manual review follows. You can [appeal](#appeals). |

## Appeals

If your result isn't **Approved!**, you can appeal it from **Finance → Account**:

1. Click **Submit an appeal**.
2. Answer **Why should your organization be approved?** in 50 to 5,000 characters. Include context about your business that helps the review.
3. Click **Submit Appeal**.

Polar reviews your appeal automatically first. This usually takes about a minute.

- **Appeal approved:** payment access is restored, and you can accept payments.
- **Appeal denied:** click **Ask for human review** to open a conversation with Polar's review team. Explain why the decision is wrong. Polar's team replies within one to two business days, and you can attach files to your replies.

The conversation stays on **Finance → Account**. A badge on **Finance** shows when there's an unread reply. Contacting support doesn't speed up a review or give you extra status updates.

You can submit one appeal per review.

## Organization statuses

Your organization's status decides what it can do. In the dashboard, **Finance → Account** shows where your review stands, and the **Overview** page shows a banner while the organization is not yet approved, denied or leaving Polar. In the API, it's the `status` field on the [organization](/api-reference/current/organizations/get-organization).

| Status | Meaning | Payments | Payouts |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `created` | Not yet approved | No | No |
| `active` | Approved | Yes | Yes |
| `review` | Under review | Yes | Requested payouts are held until approval |
| `snoozed` | Under review, with the review postponed | Yes | Requested payouts are held until approval |
| `denied` | Not approved. You can [appeal](#appeals). | No | No |
| `offboarding` | Leaving Polar. See [Migrate away](/migrate-away). | Existing subscriptions keep renewing | No |
| `offboarded` | Offboarding complete | No | Yes, to withdraw the remaining balance |
| `blocked` | Blocked for fraud or abuse | No | No |

## Continuous reviews

Polar keeps monitoring every transaction to prevent fraud, and reviews organizations again as their sales grow. Changing your products can also trigger a review. Most reviews don't need anything from you.

While your organization is under review, your customers aren't affected: new purchases go through and subscriptions renew as usual. You can still request a [payout](/features/finance/payouts). It shows as **Held for review** until the review is approved, then it's paid out automatically.

A continuous review checks two things:

- Your use case is still within the AUP and matches what was approved in your first review.
- Your account is financially healthy: refunds, disputes and risk scores across recent transactions.

If you verified a [domain](/features/domains), keep its DNS record in place. Polar checks it again during reviews.

## Operational guidelines

### Expected responsiveness

You're responsible for supporting your own customers. The volume of support requests Polar receives about your organization, and how you handle them when Polar loops you in, both count in reviews.

When Polar includes you in a customer support thread, reply within **48 hours**. If you don't, Polar may refund the affected customers and issue a warning. Repeated unresponsiveness leads to offboarding.

### Test transactions

Don't run test purchases with real card details. Payment processors flag this as card testing and can block the card or your account. It also triggers a review.

Use the [sandbox](/integrate/sandbox) instead. To check something in production, use a free product or a 100% discount code so no real money changes hands.

### Chargeback management

Card networks such as Visa and Mastercard consider a dispute rate above 0.7% of sales excessive, and can respond with monitoring programs, penalties and termination. Polar holds organizations to a **0.4% chargeback rate**. Customers can file a chargeback up to 120 days after a transaction, so Polar monitors it continuously.

If your rate climbs toward the threshold, Polar contacts you first to bring it down together. If that doesn't work, Polar may, in order of severity:

1. Refund individual transactions.
2. Hold payouts until the 120-day chargeback window for those transactions has closed.
3. Pause future payments.
4. Block the account and refund customers.

Polar also receives early dispute signals from the card networks. Below a certain transaction value, it refunds the order automatically, cancels any related subscription and revokes the customer's benefits. You get an email when this happens, unless you turned off **Prevented Chargebacks** in your [notifications](/features/notifications). Benefits the customer already used, such as downloaded files or forked repositories, can't be taken back.

### Policy violations

When an organization violates the AUP, separately from dispute issues, Polar offboards it. Payment processing is blocked and payouts are held pending review.

If Polar suspects fraud or intentional abuse, it blocks the account immediately. Otherwise, it contacts you and gives you 48 hours to respond. If you don't, affected customers may be refunded. Polar may also run test transactions to check an account.

For compliance and risk reasons, Polar has to cancel subscriptions and refund payments made in violation of the policy.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why is my account under review again?">
    Polar reviews organizations again as their sales grow, and when their products change. See [continuous reviews](#continuous-reviews). This is standard practice across payment platforms.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why do you need my social media links?">
    They help verify that you're a real business or creator with an online presence, and help Polar understand what you sell. Adding them can speed up your review.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are my social media links public?">
    No. Only Polar's review team sees them.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why do you ask for a 100% discount code or a video of my product?">
    To check that your product works and is delivered automatically after purchase, the review team may ask for a 100% discount code by email. It lets them go through the full journey from unpaid to paid user themselves.

    Alternatively, you can send a video that shows the complete flow from an unpaid user to a paid user, including how the product becomes available after purchase.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I transfer ownership of my organization?">
    See [Team management](/features/team-management#transfer-ownership).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
