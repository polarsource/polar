Polar charges a fee on each transaction, at your plan's rate. The free **Starter** plan has no monthly fee. The paid plans, **Pro**, **Growth** and **Scale**, add a monthly fee in exchange for a lower rate and prioritized support. You can switch plans at any time, and your rate changes immediately.

## Plans

| Plan | Monthly fee | Per transaction | Support | Included |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Starter** | Free | 5% + 50¢ | Standard support | |
| **Pro** | $20/mo | 3.8% + 40¢ | Prioritized support | |
| **Growth** | $100/mo | 3.6% + 35¢ | Prioritized support | |
| **Scale** | $400/mo | 3.4% + 30¢ | Slack + prioritized support | [Single Sign-On](/features/sso) |

### When does a paid plan pay off?

Each paid plan costs less than Starter once your monthly sales pass a threshold:

| Plan | Breakeven vs. Starter |
| --- | --- |
| **Pro** | ~$1,379/mo in sales |
| **Growth** | ~$5,634/mo in sales |
| **Scale** | ~$19,048/mo in sales |

Below the threshold, the lower plan is cheaper.

### Change your plan

1. Open [**Settings → Billing**](https://polar.sh/to/dashboard/settings/billing).
2. Click **Change plan**.
3. Pick a plan and confirm with **Upgrade plan**, **Downgrade plan** or **Continue to checkout**.

If you're on a custom plan, contact [support](/support) to change it.

<Note>Dashboard only. Plans can't be changed from the CLI or MCP server.</Note>

## Preview features

Paid plans include access to features that are still in preview:

- [Shared Slack Channel benefit](/features/benefits/slack-shared-channel): give each customer a shared Slack channel through Slack Connect.
- [`reset` proration](/features/subscriptions/proration#charge-full-amount-and-reset-cycle-reset): on a plan change, charge the full new price and restart the billing cycle.
- [Off-session charges](/features/off-session-charges): charge a customer's saved payment method outside a checkout or renewal.

**Testing in the sandbox?** The sandbox has no paid plans. To try preview features there, open **Settings → Billing** in your sandbox organization and click **I understand, enable preview access**. To use them in production, you'll need a paid plan, or to wait until they're generally available.

## Early Member

Organizations created before **May 27, 2026** keep the Early Member rate for as long as they stay on it:

| Monthly fee | Per transaction | Subscription fee |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | 4% + 40¢ | +0.5% |

**One trade-off:** upgrading to a paid plan retires Early Member for that organization. You can switch freely between paid plans afterwards, but downgrading takes you to Starter at 5% + 50¢, not back to Early Member.

Organizations created on or after May 27, 2026 start on Starter, even when the person creating them signed up earlier.

## Additional fees

These apply on top of your plan's rate:

- **+1.5%** for cards issued outside the US, including cards saved in Link. Other payment methods don't incur it.
- **+0.5%** for subscription payments, on Early Member only. Starter, Pro, Growth and Scale have no subscription fee.
- Polar may pass on new fees that Stripe introduces.

Fees are calculated on the total the customer pays, **tax included**.

### Example

A $30 purchase from Sweden, with 25% VAT, paid with a card issued outside the US:

| Item | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Product price | $30.00 |
| VAT (25%) | $7.50 |
| **Total paid** | **$37.50** |

The fees on that $37.50 transaction, per plan:

| Plan | Transaction fee | International (+1.5%) | Total fees |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Starter** (5% + 50¢) | \$2.38 | \$0.56 | **\$2.94** |
| **Pro** (3.8% + 40¢) | \$1.83 | \$0.56 | **\$2.39** |
| **Growth** (3.6% + 35¢) | \$1.70 | \$0.56 | **\$2.26** |
| **Scale** (3.4% + 30¢) | \$1.58 | \$0.56 | **\$2.14** |

## Refunds

You can issue full or partial [refunds](/features/refunds). The original transaction fees aren't returned to you: card networks and payment processors charge them whether or not the payment is refunded later.

Polar reserves the right to refund a purchase itself up to 60 days after it was made, to head off a likely dispute. It only does this to reduce disputes and their fees.

## Dispute fees

A customer can ask their bank to reverse a payment. This is a **dispute**, or chargeback. Each dispute costs the payment processor's dispute fee, currently $15, deducted from your balance whatever the outcome. The processor charges it either way, so Polar can't refund it. See [Disputes](/features/disputes).

Card networks penalize sellers whose dispute rate is too high, at around 0.7% of sales and above. Since Polar is the Merchant of Record, it monitors your rate and may step in, up to suspending your account, if yours stays high. See [Account reviews](/merchant-of-record/account-reviews#chargeback-management).

## Payout fees

Withdrawing your balance carries Stripe's payout fees, which Polar passes on without a markup. See [Payout fees](/features/finance/payouts#payout-fees).

## Volume pricing

The Scale plan is Polar's lowest public rate. If you need custom terms on top of it, [contact support](/support).
