Customers can buy from almost anywhere. You can be paid out in the countries Stripe Connect Express supports for Polar.

## Payments

Customers can buy from anywhere except countries under US sanctions: Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Russia and Syria.

As your [Merchant of Record](/merchant-of-record/introduction), Polar takes on the liability for sales tax in every country you sell to.

## Payouts

Stripe Payments doesn't need to be available in your country. Polar charges your customers as the Merchant of Record. You only need Stripe Connect Express to receive payouts, and it covers more countries than Stripe Payments.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I use Polar where Stripe is invite-only, such as India?">
    Yes. Customers pay Polar (US), and Polar uses Stripe Connect Express only to transfer and pay out your earnings. Connect Express payouts are available in every country listed above, even where Stripe Payments is invite-only.

    Stripe may still show a notice in your Connect Express account that payments are invite-only. You can ignore it: no sales go directly to that account.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I sell as an individual?">
    Yes, if Stripe Connect Express supports individuals in your country. To check, open Stripe's [required verification information](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/required-verification-information#US+RS+express+recipient+individual+transfers) and set:

    - **Platform Country:** United States (US)
    - **Dashboard Type:** express
    - **Service Agreement:** recipient
    - **Capability:** transfers
    - **Account Country:** your country

    The **Business Type** options then show whether individual, company or other business types are supported.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
