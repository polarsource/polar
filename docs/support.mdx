## Start with the docs

The docs cover setup, features and policies. Search them first, or ask your agent: the [MCP server](/integrate/mcp) can search and read these docs.

## From the dashboard

Click **Support** at the bottom of the sidebar in your Polar dashboard. Ask a question and get an answer drawn from the docs. If that doesn't solve it, send it to the Polar team as a **Question**, **Feedback** or **Bug**.

## Email

Email [support@polar.sh](mailto:support@polar.sh).

Polar typically replies within 24 to 48 hours on weekdays, and prioritizes questions about ongoing billing, payouts and payments. Response times can be longer when request volume is high.

Paid plans get prioritized support, and the Scale plan adds a shared Slack channel. See [Fees](/merchant-of-record/fees#plans).

## Account reviews

Account reviews happen in the dashboard, not over email. Submitting your organization runs an automated review that typically takes one to two minutes. If you disagree with the result, appeal from **Finance → Account**: Polar's review team replies there within one to two business days. Emailing support doesn't speed up a review.

See [Account reviews](/merchant-of-record/account-reviews).
